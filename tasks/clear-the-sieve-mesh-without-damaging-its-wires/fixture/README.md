# Parts and grain map

No custom part is needed. Every item is a catalogue product used at its real size; this folder only records the
dimensions the simulation uses and the map of the 20 grain sites.

- Sieve: Gilson V3SF #16, 76 mm outside diameter, 44.45 mm frame height, 1.18 mm openings, stainless frame and cloth.
  Estimates to check with a caliper and scale: 1.2 mm frame wall, wire plane 7.2 mm above the frame's bottom edge,
  0.63 mm wire at 1.81 mm pitch (the typical No. 16 values in ASTM E11), 90 g.
- Pan: Gilson V3SFXPN, 76 mm diameter. Estimates: 44.45 mm tall, 1 mm floor, 100 g.
- Brush: Gilson TSA-170 fine sieve brush, 146.1 mm long, 31.75 mm across the bristles, lacquered wood handle,
  nickeled steel ferrule. Estimates: 16 mm handle x 100 mm, 22 mm ferrule x 20 mm, 26.1 mm bristle length, 40 g.
- Surroundings: Gilson SS-3 sieve shaker (203 x 279 x 559 mm, switched off) and the counter's back wall and shelf.

[native-layout.svg](native-layout.svg) shows the products, the lot A positions and the 20 numbered grain sites:
five columns 9.05 mm apart and four rows 5.43 mm and 16.29 mm either side of the mesh centre (each site is one
opening). The same map is `SITES` in residue/__init__.py.
