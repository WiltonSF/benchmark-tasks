# Passive supports and enrollment, version6

Dimensions are millimetres. The loaded coin split happens in the hands, clear of all supports. The case is never attached to a fixture. The table supports carry it during later setdown and exchange.

Use a bought four-button HYQ14FBA / R-SHELL-79 ABS replacement shell, a quarter and inert CR2032 specimens. No custom part is consumed per trial. Make the simple supports once from stock wood/acrylic. Fix the spent tray and blocks to the mat with removable tape; leave the case/coin/cells free. Coin and replacement trays are movable, ballasted to150g.

- Mat:440x500x1, centre x320/y0.
- Two case supports:10x30x21.5, centres x300/x340,y25. Bottom on the spent-tray floor3 above the table; top24.5 above table. The nominal case bottom starts25 high and settles onto the blocks.
- Quarter tray:64x64x2 base,6-high walls. A24x12x23 pedestal has bottom2 above tray origin. Two24x3x4 rails centred y-3/+3,z27 form a3-wide slot. Quarter bottom25 above tray origin; centre38.13 above tabletop. Grasp the24.26mm rim, not its1.75mm thickness.
- Replacement tray:64x64x2 base and6-high walls;8x8x32 pedestal centred z18, top34 above tray origin. The20x3.2 cell is flat plus up with exposed rim. Nominal reset centre35.6 above table; its tray settles onto the1mm mat.
- Spent tray:110x100x2 base;3-thick,6-high walls, top9 above tabletop; inside104x94. Centre x320/y45. Case/support centres remain y25. Final old-cell centre is restricted to x +/-41 and y +/-36 from this tray centre and z<=9 above table.
