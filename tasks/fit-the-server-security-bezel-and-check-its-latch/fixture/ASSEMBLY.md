This is a specified custom prototype, not a vendor matching-part claim or a measured build. Units are mm. Parts and their assembled locations are encoded in parts.scad, regenerated by generate_cad.py from the collision solids. Set `part` in the SCAD header, render with F6, then export STL. The rack module is a fabrication/assembly reference, not a single print. CNC/print bore finishing and the drill operations below are required. No purchases or hardware trials have been performed.

Wind on a5mm mandrel or have a spring maker produce that specification; finish the closed ends parallel. Estimated shear modulus79GPa gives0.464N/mm. Record the actual free length and return behaviour; no force calibration has been claimed.

Install between the fixed seat's y159 face and collar's y179.92 face:20.92mm gives1.08mm compression, about0.5N specified preload. Full retraction leaves12.92mm spring length, above the nominal5mm solid coil height. Spring load reaches about4.21N. The physical side stops, not a hidden latch flag, bound travel. Verify the flag returns to within1mm and the cam can ride the keeper under an ordinary hand push. ## Trial preparation and check

1. Verify dry, burr-free parts and the unchanged slots/cam/guide/spring. Mark the fitted front datum x357.5 and the flag's outward position. Weigh the bezel assembly and record material/print settings.
2. Place the bezel upright on its25mm-deep bottom at(220,0), floor at table height. Use the unchanged top edge; no magnets, glue, stabilizing scaffold or extra grip tabs.
3. After installation and both hands withdrawing, apply10N outward for1second using a spring balance hooked through an ordinary central vent. Observe both ends; measure both ends/middle relative to the pre-pull face with a ruler or2mm feeler. Both tabs must remain captured, the latch flag returned within1mm, and no face point move more than2mm. This is the evaluator's retention check.
4. Reset by retracting the flag, pivoting the opposite end out, then sliding the tabs out. Check for wear or bent tabs after a failed trial. The drawing specifies a prototype; no hardware trial or measured force result is claimed.
