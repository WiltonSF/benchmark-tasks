# Specified-trainer measurement worksheet

Status: no measurements supplied. The sample file is deliberately empty. Public SOF Gen 5 measurements and estimates from PR 1026 cannot fulfill the last verdict on the C-A-T Gen 7 trainer.

Use a NAR blue C-A-T Gen 7 30-0033 on the exact 127 mm diameter training cylinder, with the enrolled band, bladder and chocks. Record the specimen/lot, date, instrument model, resolution and uncertainty, the lever distance, starting band position, cylinder circumference, gauge readings and a side photograph showing the attached factory clip. Keep people and personal details out of reusable evidence.

1. Check the force instrument against a known mass; record the result. Zero it in its measurement orientation. Check the gauge at zero and at known pressure points with a reference manometer.
2. Attach a nonstretch loop at a measured 60 mm lever from the winding axis. Pull tangentially, level with the rod. At 78, 150 and 210 degrees from the start, measure both the slow tightening peak and the static holding force in three independent resets. Record raw newtons, lever metres and pressure; torque is force times perpendicular lever. Do not average tightening resistance down with the lower holding force.
3. Measure pressure at 0, 30, 78, 150, 190 and 210 degrees. Repeat in the opposite winding direction if the task allows both directions. Record the actual curve; do not adjust physical readings to force a linear curve. Revise the task's allowed winding direction or model if the curves differ.
4. Measure insertion and extraction force directly over each factory clip hook using a push/pull force gauge at slow speed, three repeats per hook, at both an early lock and the green-range lock. Subtract the rod's no-clip support force at the same point. Record the full force/displacement curve with a ruler and video, not only a peak. A kitchen scale under the full rig is an alternative only if other applied loads are separately measured and removed.
5. Include raw CSV or a de-identified readable scan in evidence. Hash that file and fill the JSON template with observed values. The loader validates provenance and calculates upper observed winding torques and snap peaks; it does not automatically refit the physical springs. Explicitly refit and revalidate the physical winding, tilt and clip parameters before installing a completed calibration file in a calibrated version. Confirm instrument uncertainty fits the simulated tolerances. No hardware reading has been obtained by running a simulator.
6. Tune both insertion and extraction curves in the contact probe. A single assumed lip stiffness is not a force calibration. Verify reaction on the holding hand and clip supports, clip retention, bimanual handoff and the 1 s release hold in Isaac at the matched loads. Describe the model as hardware-calibrated only after those checks and independent review. The current development version uses disclosed estimates under the build instruction; missing readings remain open work, not a reason to stop simulation development.

No human application or medical efficacy is evaluated. The gauge target is a trainer indicator, not a treatment threshold.

Assessor retention confirmation: after scoring the released hold, pull upward along the clip normal at the clipped rod end with 2 N for 1 s and record any extraction or loss of seating. The fixed 2 N protocol is approximately a 200 g load and one fifth of the stated 10 N insertion prior; it is a gentle end-state check, not a measured manufacturer extraction specification. The full three-repeat extraction curves in step4 remain required for hardware calibration. The scored robot instruction and native force gates are unchanged.
