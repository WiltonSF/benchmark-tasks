# Release a newly cast soft-robot actuator from its internal core

Remove the core from the cured actuator and leave the actuator intact in the parts tray.

This is fabrication of a pressure-bearing soft robot part, before end capping. The [Soft Robotics Toolkit Step 3b](https://softroboticstoolkit.com/book/fr-plug-one-end) says “Place the end of the rod in a vise” and describes twist, IPA lubrication and supported withdrawal. The task tests preservation of the complete cast during release of a 170 mm contact interface. It makes no claim about time saved or measured AI progress. No occupation-count claim is needed for this robot-building category.

Never give overlap-progress credit. Any new tear remains 0 after reseating or returning to the start. The low-cost pressure instrument is a water U-tube on a650mm vertical board: use about1.4m of clear6.35mm-ID tubing in a U, fill with clean water to matching zero levels, one limb open to atmosphere and the other connected through an empty trap to the specimen/syringe/valve. Read the vertical difference of both menisci against a1mm ruler, not one meniscus alone. Use p=rho*g*h with water density998kg/m3 near20C and g9.81m/s2:5.0kPa corresponds to approximately511mm head,0.5kPa to51mm,0.1kPa to10mm. Record water temperature and conversion; [NIST guidance](https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/calibrations/pmc-2.pdf) cautions that water-column units require a stated density/conversion. Level/zero the board, remove bubbles, and verify readings against known column heights before testing. An empty trap separates liquid from the actuator. This replaces the earlier unspecified Dwyer MarkII suggestion, whose common low-range versions do not reach5kPa. Budget and new tubing are included above; the apparatus still requires qualification. For the witness pull check, zero the10N spring scale and verify at approximately4.9/7.8N using500/800g known masses weighed on the kit scale; withdraw the witness core along its axis with the body supported, log force/video and reject any peak above8N or sustained range below4N while most of the core remains engaged. The scale does not qualify18N damage thresholds.

Posttrial integrity is performed only after full extraction. Photograph all accessible surfaces against a 0.5 mm scale with macro focus. Smooth tapered endplugs enter no more than 5 mm, held in a padded fixture; supports prevent lifting the body by its thin wall. Connect syringe, gauge and isolation valve, raise to 5.0 kPa, wait 30 s for initial relaxation, re-establish 5.0 kPa, isolate 10 s. Loss<=0.5 kPa passes. Before and after each batch run the same plugs/gauge/tubing against a capped rigid blank: loss<=0.1 kPa in 10 s, otherwise all affected outcomes are unqualified 0. Verify witness casts before testing policy cast; check plugs for insertion tears and use video of installation. If test handling creates uncertain new damage, score 0, never repair or silently pass. This is a proposed repeatable check, not a qualified apparatus or operational pressure rating. Real trials require qualification; no real trial has been run.

## Declared physics model

The actuator's 13 rigid circumferential panels form one rigid body. This preserves its hollow semilunar section and supports/contact locations, but not silicone compliance or local strain. Core has collision geometry, bore has 0.2 mm radial solver clearance. Each 240 Hz substep computes relative core/body velocity projected on the current body axis. While geometrically overlapping, axial adhesion uses a 400 N/m elastic bristle state integrated from actual relative axial velocity each substep, with 0.2 N s/m damping and force capped at 6 N times overlap/170 mm. Sliding clamps the bristle extension to that cap; reversing can return stored elastic energy. Full-overlap 6 N corresponds to 15 mm axial extension (8.8% strain estimate). This replaces the initial memoryless tanh drag, which injected numerical chatter at 240 Hz on the light casting. Radial misalignment uses 1500 N/m capped 12 N. Equal opposite forces use a common estimated interface point (midpoint of the two part origins); moments are computed about each root-link origin. This is equivalent to co-located wrenches because the [native API applies a position-unspecified force at the link transform](https://docs.omniverse.nvidia.com/kit/docs/omni_physics/109.0/extensions/runtime/source/omni.physics.tensors/docs/api/python.html), and the composer converts global vectors to link frame. set_external_force_and_torque and write_data_to_sim are called for both bodies each substep. The axial force is collinear; radial-foundation application at that estimated point is an approximation to distributed contact. Applied-force failure latches zero. No 90-degree unlock, no pose-only credit, no teleports, no grasp constraints. The exposed 50 mm core handle is dry steel with estimated static/dynamic friction 0.8/0.6 against rubber pads; the inserted 170 mm core section uses 0.12/0.10. Separating these surfaces fixes an earlier failed low-friction handle grip, without changing dimensions. Native PhysX contacts also act; values are engineering class estimates within the research 4–8 N lubricated range, not material measurements. Maximum axial term 6 N; combined magnitude can reach 13.42 N. The initial memoryless law is archived as a failed approach; only the bristle law is used in later trials. Work/force/pose traces qualify simulation behavior only.

Tear proxy permanently zeros for >4 mm transverse misalignment or>15 deg axis error while>10 mm engaged, sustained core contact resultant>18 N for 0.1 s or inferred axial traction (core mass times measured acceleration minus interface force)>18 N for 0.1 s, sustained body squeezing>45 N for 0.1 s, or an unsupported thin cut-rim pinch within 5 mm of an end (>2 N on each opposing fingertip of one hand), or any single rim contact>8 N. A one-sided brush of 3 N is not a grip. [Wacker cured-material data](https://www.wacker.com/h/en-us/medias/ELASTOSIL-M-4601-AB-en-2025.03.07.pdf) gives density 1.13 g/cm³, tensile strength 6.5 N/mm 2 and tear strength>30 N/mm after 24 h at 23 C. The nominal 2 mm wall therefore has a laboratory tear-force scale>60 N;8 N single-contact threshold is a conservative factor>7.5 estimate, not a conversion to an assembled-actuator rupture prediction. Opposed thin-rim pinching remains prohibited at 2 N each because compression/notches can damage the pressure wall. Native contact reports showed 3–4 N on one finger and 0 on the opposite finger during a brush while the core was gripped; the earlier any-touch 2 N rule conflated these cases. Existing zero scores are retained; only fresh runs use the corrected rule. These specimen properties do not qualify actual cured, reinforced or lubricated casts. The inferred traction excludes unmeasured axial components of native core/body contact and is an estimate, not a load cell. These limits are conservative estimates requiring cast/coupon validation; they do not predict microscopic leaks. Broad native reinforced body gripping is permitted; cutting rims cannot be pinched. A strong straight axial extraction can pass legitimately; twist/two-hand method is never compulsory. Real one-hand extraction may also succeed; no claim of uniquely bimanual ability follows without the countercheck.

The open-loop demo uses only published body/core positions and visible tray layout. The right wrist leans 25 degrees with fingers toward+y so its proximal finger surfaces clear the body rim; bundledtilt.py extends only scripted waypoint orientation. The left counterhold takes up 12 mm of measured arm deflection with an open-loop motion. Neither helper reads hidden state to choose actions. No hidden force state or pose feedback directs it. Layouts reduce a single tray-location habit; memorized replay of both can pass.
