Analytical and teaching laboratory staff prepare a known, air-free volume before weighing a liquid for density. Category: **Other**. This is sample/reference preparation, with uses across product families. [ISO 2811-1:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/83323.html) specifies pycnometer density measurement for paints, varnishes and related products. [ASTM D1475](https://store.astm.org/standards/d1475) covers density of liquid paints, inks, varnishes and lacquers. These establish the procedure's cross-sector use; they do not establish the number of manual fills, workers, or laboratories using this exact bottle.

## Declared fluid rule and its limits

There is **no water or air fluid solver**. `fluid.Water` conserves water inventory. An open immersed neck admits water at a depth-dependent estimated10 mL/s only when its actual mouth is above the inner bulb's highest point, so an inverted vessel cannot acquire a free fill. Open-bottle retained volume is computed from3 mm inner-cavity samples below the lowest mouth-rim gravity plane;water above that capacity drains at up to12 mL/s,including tilts below the old fixed angle threshold; a seated stopper with blocked capillary cannot drain. A dry open capillary drains seated glass when its actual outlet lies below the interior water: a gravity plane through that outlet bounds retained volume, and Poiseuille flow (0.7 mm estimated radius, 52 mm length, water viscosity1 mPa·s) estimates leakage. Its loss is caught at that actual outlet and never earns insertion-overflow credit. Detailed headspace compression and two-phase exchange are omitted; this conservative leakage approximation needs physical qualification.

Each of twelve0.10 mL pockets starts67° from the local top. Its buoyant force is approximately0.979 mN; the upright wall-tangent component is0.901 mN. A pocket depins when its **own actual tangential buoyancy** exceeds the estimated0.970 mN per0.10 mL contact-line resistance. Over the published0.09–0.11 mL preparation range, resistance scales with each pocket's current volume, retaining the nominal0.991 resistance/buoyancy ratio. This is a local reduced-order wetting assumption for enrolled specimens, not a universal capillary scaling law. An absolute0.970 mN threshold would make an admitted0.095 mL pocket impossible to depin; that mismatch is removed and the full volume range is now a causal control. This is below the approximate1.30 mN surface-tension upper bound from0.072 N/m times the perimeter of a5.76 mm circular contact. Actual wetting hysteresis is unmeasured. Each freed pocket travels independently along the wall with estimated0.025 N·s/m wall drag and50 mm/s speed cap. Its vacated volume is replaced by bath water. A pose or seating event cannot delete air.
