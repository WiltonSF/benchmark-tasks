# Fixture revision 3 — dimensions in millimetres

This is a custom cord saddle mounted in a Hammond 1550G enclosure. The manufacturer does not specify or certify this assembly. Dimensions below define the fabricated pieces; maker dimensions apply only to the named stock products. Drawing revision 3 matches the Python primitives. No physical specimen has been made or calibrated. General fabrication tolerance ±0.1 mm; clamp holes ±0.05 mm, threads ISO metric standard fit; bar face flatness 0.05 mm.

Datum: enclosure floor underside z=0; x across the long box dimension, y toward the terminals. Saddle center x=0,y=0 maps to world (280,10) mm. Enclosure footprint center maps to (280,15) mm.

## BOM and machining

1. Hammond 1550G aluminum enclosure, lid removed. Outer 222 x 146 mm; nominal height 51 mm from the product listing. The drawing labels 51.1 mm inside height and 55.1 mm in section A, versus 51 mm in section B; verify the stock lidless height before machining. The model uses nominal 51 mm walls and approximates casting draft with 4 mm walls. The drawing gives a 2 mm cast floor: add a reusable 214 x 138 x 2 mm aluminum liner, with 10 x 10 mm corner notches to clear the cast cover bosses, to obtain the modeled 4 mm work-floor stack. Drill the specified mounting holes through both layers. The liner is a permanent fixture part, not a per-trial specimen. Use maker hole locations for cover bosses, not for the saddle.
2. Saddle, 6061 aluminum: 52 x 16 mm footprint, bottom z=4, first block 18 mm tall (top z=22); collision geometry leaves clearance around the constrained threaded shanks. Two M4x0.7 tapped through holes at (x,y)=(-18,0),(+18,0); initial thread engagement 10.7 mm (head underside z=36.3 minus 25 mm shank gives tip z=11.3, versus saddle top z=22), increasing during tightening; at least 8 mm is maintained. Break exposed edges 0.3 mm. Cable bears on the central top face. The two M4 screw shanks guide the bar. Stock cable is cylindrical; do not machine a flat cable stand-in. Surface finish Ra 3.2 micrometres or better, deburr fully.
3. Bar, low-carbon steel: 50 x 14 x 3 mm; holes Ø4.6 through at (-18,0),(+18,0), centered across width. Flat parallel faces, deburr edges with radius 0.3 mm. Nominal volume after holes 2.0 cm3, about 16.5 g. No fine teeth or cutting ribs; clamping friction is estimated rather than implied by sharp ribs. Loose underside initially z=33.0, held by captive shanks above 10.9 mm cable whose nominal top is z=32.9. The 0.1 mm gap falls closed under gravity.
4. Two M4x25 DIN933 external-hex steel screws: 7 mm AF, nominal head height 2.8 mm (model 3.2 mm). Keep both started in the tapped holes; retain for reset. Head underside z=36.3 initially (0.4 mm above nominal seated bar top). Screw thread pitch 0.7 mm. Model exposed shank has its full 25 mm length. Collision bores in the saddle are Ø4.6 proxies for the tapped holes; the thread constraint supplies the mechanical fit. Use washers only if drawings and start gap are updated consistently.
5. Four M3x20 DIN7991 countersunk screws mount the saddle from beneath the enclosure floor. Drill Ø3.4 clearance holes in the 2 mm cast floor plus 2 mm liner at x=±22,y=±5; countersink the floor underside so heads are flush. Drill Ø2.5 and tap M3 through the saddle at those same locations. Each 20 mm screw traverses the 2 mm cast floor plus 2 mm liner and engages 16 mm of the 18 mm saddle; no underside nut or protrusion is present. Mount the enclosure to a 245 x 170 x 3 mm steel pad with four M4x6 pan-head screws from inside the box, at x=±95,y=±55 relative to the enclosure footprint center. Drill Ø4.5 clearance in the 2 mm cast floor plus 2 mm liner and tap M4 in the pad; 6 mm screw length gives 2 mm steel engagement and stops 1 mm short of the pad underside. This is permanent workholding for the 30 N trial load, not a lifting attachment. Seat the pad in a 245 x 170 x 3 mm shallow bench pocket so its top is flush with the tabletop datum, and clamp its exposed perimeter to the bench; this one-time pocket keeps the below-datum pad from changing the declared heights. All assembly dimensions use the pad top as z=0; the pad extends below that datum. The simulator fixes these permanent connections to the world.
6. Terminal anchor: insulating nylon block, 50 x 18 x 25 mm, centered at world (280,69), bottom z=4. Three standard M4 terminal screws on 16 mm pitch secure the already-connected insulated-core ends, stripped only under the terminal plates. Mount the block at x=±20,y=0 relative to its center using two M3x30 socket-head screws into tapped M3 holes through the 3 mm steel pad. Drill Ø3.4 clearance through the block and 4 mm enclosure floor; counterbore Ø6, 2 mm deep, from the block top z=29. The screw head underside is then z=27; 30 mm length ends flush at pad underside z=-3, giving 3 mm steel engagement without a protruding nut. Circuit disconnected throughout. Model colors are identifiers, not live-voltage cues.
7. Prysmian H05VV-F 3G4 cord: cut a 295 mm piece and remove 65 mm of outer sheath from one end, leaving 230 mm sheathed tail. Do not nick individual insulation. The three insulated cores remain connected to the anchor; their installed bow provides the outward restoring load. Nominal 10.9 mm OD, 215 g/m. Measure actual OD and record lot; reject <10 mm OD for this robot grasp. Model uses rigid 230 mm tail; actual external tail is unsupported except at the entry and gripper. Entry opening is 16 x 16 mm, center z=27.45, at the middle of the enclosure's right wall. Fit a smooth 0.5 mm grommet edge or deburr; verify cable slides freely at reset. No cable collar or robot-only grip adapter.
8. Driver: Wera 811/1 Stubby, 65 mm overall (54 mm handle), plus 869/4 M 7x50 mm nutsetter with an estimated 10 mm insertion. Envelope 105 mm overall by 65+50-10; verify with a caliper before enrollment. Holder 44 g plus nutsetter 20 g gives 64 g, from maker datasheets. Estimate handle 34 mm across corners, grip band z=60-100 from socket mouth; socket OD11, 7.2 AF inside, cavity depth 7. Check actual socket/head fit with stock screw before fabrication. There are six physical socket faces; no enlarged capture zone. Machine the reusable bit once to a 1.5 mm deep entrance taper, 8.8 mm AF at the mouth to 7.2 mm AF above, within the 11 mm OD. This is a specified modification, not a maker-certified stock chamfer; inspect six flats and use the dimensioned profile in driver-profile.svg. It supplies physical contact centering, with no virtual capture. Socket must descend over the head before a wrist stroke can transmit torque.
9. Two tool rests, acetal, each 40 x 40 x 51 mm; central square through opening 20 x 20 mm; four sides forming the opening, wall thickness 10 mm. Rest centers (155,185),(215,205) mm in world x,y. Driver collar rests on top; socket mouth is spawned 2 mm above table and settles to table level. Attach rests by two M3 screws each outside the opening. Both rests exist in both lots; only driver start rest changes.
10. Tool-return tray: 200 x 200 x 15 mm, acetal or PETG, 3 mm floor, 4 mm walls rising another 12 mm. Inner floor 192 x 192 mm, centered at world (200,360) mm. Deburr or round the wall edges 0.5 mm; bolt to the bench by four M3 screws through the outer corners. The tool is placed and released inside this tray after use; its pickup stands remain for reset. Allow US$10 within the fixture material allowance.
11. Ruler, 0.2 mm feeler, 0-50 N spring scale (1 N graduations or better), marker, depth gauge and dial indicator for preparation/scoring. Use the dial indicator to resolve the 0.1 mm post-proof movement criterion; do not support the assembly with measuring fixtures during manipulation. Planning cost allowance US$55, combined with terminal hardware.

## Cable enrollment template

Fix a paper ruler along the cable axis on the floor, zero at bar center. Mark -1, +5, +12 and +18 mm. +12 means sheath at least 5 mm beyond the bar's terminal-side edge y=+7. Route the three 65 mm insulated cores in parallel upward bows from the sheath end to their terminal screws, bend radii at least 12 mm on the individually insulated cores; the sheathed 230 mm tail is straight in the entry, so the catalogue bend radius is not violated.

With screws loose, push the sheath edge to +18 mm and measure force using the 0-50 N scale attached temporarily outside the entry. Accept 3-6 N at that position. On release the edge must return to 0..+5 mm within 2 seconds, three consecutive checks. Adjust only the unscored core bows or choose a replacement cut specimen; never use extra springs, weights or powered fixtures. Return to -1 mm before trial. A setup that cannot meet these checks is not enrolled. This is a proposed physical enrollment procedure; its yield and damage effects have not been measured.

## Observable acceptance and damage

Before and after a physical trial, photograph the sheath edge, all exposed insulated cores and the clamp contact patch under bright light. A visible slit/split or exposed copper away from the terminal is a rejected trial/specimen, not a turn or force prediction. Inspect the patch after loosening only after recording final coverage, seating, levelness and proof pull. No numerical crush/damage threshold is supplied or claimed validated. If a physical trial needs dielectric or conductor-continuity certification, it is outside this simulator's claimed measurements.

All reusable pieces are fabricated once. Planned reset/rebow allowance is 2-4 minutes, not yet measured; replacing an already-cut stock specimen takes minutes. Figure in assembly.svg shows the dimensions and the +12 mm coverage threshold; assembly.scad is a simple fabrication reference, not a copied manufacturer CAD model.

## Gravity-settled start used for the evaluations

The 0.4 mm gap above the nominal level seated-bar reference is a construction dimension. The free bar is not constrained level at reset. Native settling produces approximately 9 degrees roll about world X (across the enclosure; cable axis is Y) and approximately 0.58 mm head gaps in both lots; restore that loose pose using the setup image and feeler, with screw-head underside datum 36.3 mm. Initial native gaps and screw angles are recorded in the evaluation trace. The sheath's nominal -1 mm reset settles to approximately -1.19 mm. These are observed simulator starting states, not measurements of a manufactured assembly. Matching physical enrollment remains required before hardware trials.

Proposed physical start enrollment tolerance: each head gap 0.58 +/- 0.03 mm; free-bar roll about world X -9 +/- 1 degrees; initial sheath edge -1.19 +/- 0.1 mm. These numerical start checks do not replace the separate force/spring-back enrollment. Native gap values are logged, not latched as a new score threshold; hardware tolerance validation remains pending.
