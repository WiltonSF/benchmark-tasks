# Fit the gearbox output lip seal square and flush without cutting its lip

A new radial lip seal is fitted over a keyed output shaft into a gearbox seal carrier. Protection of the lip, square loaded seating and the flush stop are the scored work. The complete gearbox exterior and vise constrain access. This is industrial machinery maintenance on an isolated repair bench.

The seating region means the case centre is within 25mm horizontally of the shaft axis and its lower-face centre is between 15mm below and 20mm above the housing face. Applied-load samples outside that region are zero in the three-sample load history. The seating-load monitor takes the greatest available axial channel per 20Hz point sample in that region, then a three-sample mean including the zero samples outside it. It includes hand-to-driver normal force plus tool/seal weight, so load diverted to the carrier stop is not invisible. While the case is in the seating region, both hands must stay 5mm clear of it under a>=4N three-sample mean load; direct hand loading there invalidates the trial.
