# Physical scoring record

This is a proposed procedure, not measured hardware validation. One-time fixture manufacture and bearing-compound cure are separate from trial preparation. The complete reset is estimated2–3minutes: remove carrier along the sleeve, inspect the old lip, extract/replace the stock seal, clean/lubricate, reattach carrier, inspect clearances, put seal and tools on published rests and tare. This estimate is unmeasured; do not omit removal/rebuilding time.

Build the two isolated load paths in DRAWINGS.md. [Adafruit4543](https://www.adafruit.com/product/4543) gives20kg cells and requires installed-weight calibration; its [mounting drawing](https://cdn-shop.adafruit.com/product-files/4543/4543_diagram.jpg) gives75x12.7x12.7 and four M4 holes. A supports only the housing/carrier/vise. The shaft, bearing cup and guide sleeve are independently supported from B's total frame. B carries both paths. No carrier bolt or bearing face may bridge them. Test isolation before enrollment:10N applied to the sleeve should enter B with<=0.5N on A;10N on the carrier should enter both within0.5N. Stop and correct any bypass. Calibrate both channels at0/10/20/30N to<=0.5N installed error and inspect the specified clearances under load. Use two HX711 boards at80samples/s and one Pico. Tare the fixed assembly with the sleeve mounted, before placing the seal and driver; record actual seal weight.

The native rule uses one end-of-control-step contact-force sample at20Hz (the final240Hz physics-step impulse divided by1/240s), then a three-sample mean. Hardware takes every fourth phase-aligned80Hz sample, rather than averaging an entire20Hz interval. Synchronize both channels and two video views with a logged Pico LED pulse. State the80Hz acquisition and up-to6.25ms nearest-sample timing uncertainty; no continuous-force or identical ADC bandwidth claim is made. Test sparse and sustained load pulses before enrollment; uncertain transients earn no work. The seating region means the case centre is within25mm horizontally of the shaft axis and its lower-face centre is between15mm below and20mm above the housing face. Applied-load samples outside that region are zero in the three-sample load history. Over30N on that three-sample seating-load signal for150ms invalidates the trial; it is not a universal handling-force damage threshold. Use the video geometry to gate samples outside the seating region to zero. Lip-exposure and hand-lip intrusion checks apply throughout handling. Native applied load is the greater of spring reaction, driver–seal contact and measured hand-to-driver axial load plus seal/driver weight. Physical B supplies total applied load, including guide and carrier-stop paths; A supplies case reaction during unambiguous insertion. Missing/nonfinite time or a required20Hz observation invalidates history permanently.

Use two calibrated orthogonal close-up scoring views outside the workspace, at least60fps, using benchmark cameras; their cost is excluded alongside robot/cameras. Track both driver edges in both views, shaft axis and carrier face against a metric grid. A50mm field across640pixels gives<=0.079mm/pixel; verify<=0.1mm position error with a known1mm displacement. Reconstruct driver lower-face centre<=1mm from seal centre and axis<=1deg from vertical; use its measured50mm length and both-view tilt. Confirm driver–case contact (<=0.5mm mean-face gap) visibly. Occlusion or ambiguity earns no work; do not fill gaps by optimistic interpolation.

Positive depth is:
`depth = carrier_face_z - (driver_lower_face_z - measured_seal_width)`.
For a7mm seal: driver lower face at the carrier face means+7mm depth;7.6mm above it means-0.6mm depth. Track actual seal motion when visible and verify contact before using driver displacement as its proxy.

Before work is credited, require A's current sample lower confidence bound minus seal weight>=12N as a conservative current-contact check, in addition to A's three-sample lower confidence bound minus seal weight>=12N and B's upper confidence bound<=30N. With guides sliding downward and no other contact, A minus seal weight is a conservative lower bound on driver–seal force; B bounds its upper force including guide friction. Both tracked edges must move downward during credited travel; negative/ambiguous B-A, rocking, external case/carrier contact or uncertain guide-force direction earns no work. Native additionally requires current measured driver–case contact>=12N; it separately requires12–30N support and driver contact and12–30N total tool load. This conservative physical acceptance uses measurement uncertainty instead of claiming perfect channel equivalence.

The38mm driver can divert force directly into the carrier at flush. Exclude every interval from work credit when its lowest rim is<=0.2mm above the carrier face, or when contact is ambiguous. The native work gate uses the same0.2mm clearance. Keep monitoring total B for overload at the stop; visible contact alone never establishes a12N seal load there. Final released depth, not stop force, determines flushness.

Export synchronized20Hz rows: time_s, A_N, B_N, depth_m, contact_visible, lip_protected, square_mm, driver_pose_valid, stop_clear. Track deepest physical depth continuously, even when unqualified. Add support_force*max(0,depth-previous_deepest) only when all qualification conditions and stop clearance hold; update previous_deepest regardless. Repeated pressure adds0J. Thresholds0.015J/0.060J must be met conservatively; if0.1mm tracking or0.5N force uncertainty straddles a threshold, no credit is established—repeat with closer calibrated observation. Force history plus final depth is insufficient.

After release, sweep a bridge depth gauge around the full rim for extrema. Spread<=0.5mm and all heights±0.3mm of face define square/flush; concentricity<=0.5mm, white OUT face up, tools returned, hands5mm clear and every body marker within0.5mm over1s are also required. During handling keep hands clear of the lip; native swept actual hand hulls use a conservative1.5mm lip envelope with a documented sampling bound. After scoring, remove carrier/seal together along the sleeve and inspect under10x magnification; never repair damage within a trial.
