**Build status: native scripted A1.0/B1.0 and Dream-1 A0.0/B0.0 on runtime13862c3c.** Earlier same-core PR3979 is disclosed in the main comparison. Fabrication, stiffness and recorded-envelope screens are analytical; no hardware is assembled or calibrated.

This proposed fixture has not been fabricated or tested on hardware. It preserves the RM2125 RTS specimen-change surfaces and declared operating forces without buying a microtome. ## Force check and enrolment

`python fixture/clearance_check.py` reproduces continuous frame/fitting/guide-relief clearance and washer-inclusive hardware envelopes.

`python fixture/force_check.py` computes geometry, not an empirical measurement. With theta in radians, vertical follower travel is `40*tan(theta)+8*(sec(theta)-1)` mm, rather than simply40*sin(theta). At15 degrees it is11.000 mm. Virtual-work torque is `0.736*(1.0+travel)*(40*sec(theta)^2+8*sec(theta)*tan(theta))/1000` Nm. Divide by the horizontal foot force's moment arm, `(101*cos(theta)-40*sin(theta))/1000` m: **4.567 N**. Each spring carries4.416 N and compresses6.000 mm; its9.27 N solid load is not approached. A±10% rate assumption plus0.30 N gravity allowance remains within3.5–5.5 N before guide friction. Guide friction and fabrication tolerances are not predicted: measure the complete assembly and adjust preload for enrolment. The conservative native drive is1.4 Nm/rad plus0.06 Nm preload, equivalent to4.891 N at15 degrees before gravity. Actual enrolled force must be measured: do not substitute arithmetic for a spring-scale test. Fine-tune with0.2 mm shims; require at least20 mm open spring-seat gap.

Before every session, check59 mm full stroke, cassette centre175.25±1 mm initially and190±1 mm at top; wheel6–9 N turning, lock3–6 N axial and10 N tangential proof; clamp15 degrees at3.5–5.3 N horizontally at the foot and10.84±1 mm lip-bottom lift; cassette retained through one unlocked wheel rotation; full guard coverage and2 mm clearance; cassette mass/dimensions; fixture rigidity and hand access. A cord/pulley converts the pull scale into a push test. Exclude any failed fixture. Hold the lever with one hand and withdraw the cassette with the other; check no support, spring frame or housing obstructs the hands.

Reset estimate: under two minutes. Pull the lock out, return wheel60 degrees before top, lower guard, open clamp, reseat the same cassette, release clamp, put tray on its lot outline. No fresh material or curing. This estimate, assembly tolerances and measured operating forces remain unvalidated on hardware.
