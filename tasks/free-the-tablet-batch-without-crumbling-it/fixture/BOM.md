The educational masses have no precision certificate on the opened listing. Independently verify them on a calibrated lab balance before using them as force references; the balance/certification service is excluded above. A listed mass alone does not establish assembled force accuracy. Required calibration residual/repeatability is<=0.03 N; failure leaves the kit unqualified. A steel straightedge/ruler, cutters, screwdrivers,4 mm hex key, soldering iron, fine abrasive and numbering pen are ordinary workshop equipment, excluded explicitly rather than silently priced at zero.
