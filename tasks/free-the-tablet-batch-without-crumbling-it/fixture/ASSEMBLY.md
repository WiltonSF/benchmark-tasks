# Proposed RDT60-R1 assembly and enrollment

Design and qualification protocol, not a built kit or measured result. Millimetres unless stated. Torpac dimensions define a true-size cavity layout; reusable resin parts replace baked pharmaceutical specimens. This task measures force-limited push-out, not pharmaceutical fracture equivalence.

The nominal SLA tolerance is coarser than the 0.04 mm gap and 0.1 mm recess. Do not rely on printing those fits directly: finish matched parts once, use rings to set retention, and verify the stop gauge to set seating.

1. Order the watertight STLs in Tough 1500 resin, washed/cured with supports removed; original CC BY4.0 geometry. Order four copies of the 30-puck sprue. The reset uses an ordinary square counter edge with at least 80 mm clear below the overhanging grip/cap. Check every bounding box against the 200 x 125 x 210 Form 4 envelope. Save the vendor's actual quotation; the supplied rate-card arithmetic is not that quotation.
2. Install sixty silicone 5 ID x 1-section rings in the split plate's 8.2-diameter,1.1-high grooves, centred 1.65 below the upper face (lower groove edge 2.2 below it). Use six M2 bolts through the two plate halves; four M3 bolts join the 20 mm half-lap handle extension. Do not glue a ring into the bore. A 3-hole coupon permits fit/force checks before assembling the full plate. Nominal printed dimensions alone do not establish a force fit.
3. The handle is a 30 x 30 x 55 rectangular tube,2 mm walls, open alongx. The gripper pinches its oppositey faces. Before enrollment, support the loaded plate using that grip, push the far corner at 0.45 N and check that it remains level within 5 degrees and clear of every parking support by at least 0.5 mm. Reject a grip that slips or lets the plate rest on a bar. Do not alter the force/pose limits to admit it.
4. Finish the once-manufactured pucks with flush cutters and fine abrasive; remove sprues and burrs, then inspect at 10 x. Invert the plate about its long y axis onto a clean, flat, square-edged counter. Align the plate edge at y=76 with the counter edge so the connector and handle hang outside, with at least 80 mm clear below. The full cavity matrix must remain supported and flat; never balance it on a corner. Seat the narrow puck faces flush against the counter. Return upright onto the bars, then press each face with the supplied 20 mm stop gauge: its 4 mm central boss projects 0.1 mm and its collar stops on the plate lands, setting the recess without a thin sheet under the plate. Gauge the delivered boss projection with the caliper and accept 0.10 +/-0.03 mm before use; finish or reject a bad gauge. Return the plate upright onto the bars. Use a 0.1 mm recess gauge: every face must be 0.1 mm below the plate face; no puck may fall under its own weight. Do not bake or cast a fresh batch for each trial.

## Instrumented tool

Proposed TAL220B-5 kg beam (SparkFun SEN14729):55 x 12.7 x 12.7, two M5 end holes 40 apart. The higher range replaces the earlier 500 g candidate, which could not cover 6 N. Its published combined error 0.05%FS corresponds to about 0.0245 N; this is a datasheet bound, not assembled accuracy.

The fixed upper grip/frame bolts to the beam atx=-40, with a 3 mm spacer above that end. The lower moving card and integral 1.8 mm pin bolt below the beam atx=0. The pin ends atz=-47 relative to the nominal 70 mm card centre. The rest of the lower card clears the beam/frame by at least 0.8 mm; the hollow cover belongs only to the fixed end. Every intended pin/lower-card contact load must cross the beam. Never clamp the beam across both ends or let the cover touch the moving carrier. Use two M5 x 10 socket cap screws (8.72 maximum head diameter, 5 maximum head height) into the drawing's M5 threaded end holes, with 7 mm engagement through each 3 mm spacer. Verify the delivered beam has those threads; reject a mismatched part. The central slot widens to 12 x 10.5 to clear the lower screw head. Assembly geometry is provided for inspection, not a claim that this mounting has been manufactured or tested.

Connect red/black toHX711E+/E-, green/white toA+/A-. Adafruit HX7115974 offers pre-soldered terminals and 10/80 SPS selection. Select 80 SPS; connect 3.3V logic/power,ground,clock/data to a Pico USB logger, with strain relief attached to the fixed frame and a slack wire loop. Retain timestamped raw ADC samples as well as calibratedN. Physical logging is 80 Hz, unlike the 240 Hz simulation monitor. Missing samples, saturation, rubbing, temperature drift or fixed-frame contact invalidate a physical trial. Video must show that only the instrumented lower card/pin contacts workpieces.

Calibrate in the actual downward grip with independently checked 10,20,50,100,500 g masses at the pin tip, using 9.80665 m/s² to convert toN. Fit offset/slope, verify both rising and falling loads, and retain raw readings. Require residual and repeatability <=0.03 N over 0.1-6 N, no contact between carrier and frame, and no change exceeding 0.03 N after moving the slack lead. Tare before each trial. These acceptance criteria have not been measured. If the cheap sensor/ADC cannot meet them, the kit is unqualified; do not assert 0.40 N resolution from ADC bit count alone.

## Puck qualification and reset

Qualify every puck in its assigned cavity using the actual polymer pin and a slow axial push. Require peak release force 0.40-0.45 N, approximately 2.2 mm engagement before freedom, initial face 0.1 mm below the plate face and no chips/cracks >=0.5 mm. Repeat three complete load/release cycles per position. Reject/refit an outlier; never lowerF_REL=0.40 N or raiseF_TAB=4 N to fit a bad specimen. The simulation's 0.45 N resisting cap is an unmeasured conservative stand-in, not measured ring friction. Account for calibrated force uncertainty: the entire corrected interval must lie inside the enrollment band, not only the displayed value. The logger precheck alone does not certify a puck. Record puck/cavity IDs and measured traces. Stop a real trial above 4 N on one puck or 6 N aggregate. Reinspect after each trial; damaged pucks are replaced from the once-manufactured spares and requalified.

For each reset: gather the sixty numbered pucks, inspect, invert at the qualified counter edge, reseat, put parchment/bars/parking block at the declared coordinates, check faces/grip/clearance, and tare/photograph. Estimated 3-5 minutes after initial qualification; no timed physical reset has been performed. Inspection equipment and its cost boundaries are listed separately in [BOM.md](BOM.md).

## Logger software reference

Use the maker's [CircuitPython HX711 guide](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-hx711-24-bit-adc/circuitpython) and `Adafruit_CircuitPython_HX711` library. For the Pico, connect GP2 to DATA, GP3 to SCK, 3.3 V to VIN and ground to GND. Select channel A at gain 128 and the board's 80SPS switch. Record a monotonic timestamp and each reported count to the USB serial log; omit the example's one-second display delay. Verify actual sample intervals during calibration and reject missing samples or gaps over 30ms. ADC bit depth alone is not a force-resolution measurement. Save the original counts, calibration loads/fit/residuals, tare and derived newtons together. This wiring/software configuration has not been tested on hardware here.


The current pin is 1.8 mm diameter with 12 mm unsupported length. This is a proposed print, not a qualified tool. As a screening calculation only, fixed-free Euler buckling with the published 1370 MPa flexural modulus and a circular section gives about 12.10 N at nominal diameter, or 7.55 N at 1.6 mm diameter. Actual layer/support defects are not captured. Before enrollment, proof-load the delivered pin axially to 6 N, inspect for permanent bend/cracks and check its diameter/straightness. Reject a failed pin; do not raise force caps or assert the calculation is a measurement. Repeat puck force qualification using this exact revised tip.

The grip includes a central 2 mm compression web joining the opposing y walls, with open x-end cavities for washing. It gives the pinch a direct resin load path. Qualify the completed grip under the actual robot pinch and far-hole push; the simulation treats the assembly as rigid and does not establish delivered stiffness.

The stop ledge is12 x44 x3, centred2 mm in positive localx from the tube axis and parallel to the cavity plate, underside57.3 mm above its back. Its12 x12 x3.5 neck starts54 mm above the back, overlapping the55 mm tube and ledge. Pinch the tube below it. Its narrow centre clears the carriage rails and can bear on the lower inner pad surface. Confirm approach, closure, support and release against the delivered finger geometry; no hand weld or pose hold is permitted.

Finish the upper tool-block guides to a5.0 +/-0.2 mm slot. Guides rise to38 mm, giving20 mm of card engagement above the18 mm card bottom; the old20 mm block alone gives only2 mm and permits tipping. Their localX range15-45 clears the sensor cover and screw head. Verify upright rest and three free withdrawals/reseatings without the block shifting; fitting is done once, not per trial.
