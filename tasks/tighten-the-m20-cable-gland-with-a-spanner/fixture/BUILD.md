# Fixture v1 — millimetres

Use a Hammond 1591ESBK enclosure with the lid removed, x depth 110.6, y width 190.4, z height 60.8. Drill one 20.5 mm hole in its near end wall, centred along y and 35 mm above the enclosure base. Fit HUMMEL 1.209.2000.50 body and its M20 locknut. The wall is 2.4 mm nominal. Put the enclosure on a reusable 110 x 190.4 x 30 mm wood riser. Its near wall is x 255 and gland world height 65 mm. Two small padded screw clamps grip the side walls: simulated clamp feet at x 330, y=-100 and 130, extending onto the box rims. Tighten the body against its locknut to 4.5 Nm. Bolt a reusable printed 24 mm key on its body flats to the enclosure fixture to counter-hold the cap torque. Verify no body rotation over 5 degrees or enclosure movement under 4.5 Nm. The simulated fixed body represents this counter-hold; physical proof-testing remains to be done. Never use a stand to turn the nut or hold the cable for the robot.

Two cable U supports have 18 x 35 mm feet, floor 59 mm above the bench, and 10 mm lips leaving a 15 mm open top slot. Lips reach 66 mm. Smooth PETG or wood is suitable. Place centres (0, 15) and(170, 15). These support the loose cable but do not clamp it. The gripper travels behind the front support. Verify the specified cable rests with its centre at 65 +/-1 mm without a permanent clamp or hidden setup spacer.

GEDORE 894 24 /6575570 spanner: nominal 217 mm/total 215 mm, 24 mm jaw, 15-degree set, 9 mm head. The sleeve is centred 165 mm along the handle from the 24 mm jaw centre. Finished outside 24 x 60 x 50 mm; internal channel follows the actual handle, nominal 10 x 18 mm. Fabricate two hollow PETG halves with 3 mm walls/ribs and two M3 through screws, nut traps and washers, 34 mm pitch along the sleeve. Approximate sleeve mass 50 g. File mating faces flat. Proof-load the sleeve on the actual wrench with 35 N transverse force and reject slip over 1 mm. The simulation treats this bolted tool attachment as rigid, not as a weld to the gripper.

Tool rest: a 50 x 10 x 10 mm sleeve support at (215, 235); a 60 x 40 x 8.2 mm head support at (205, 40). Tool jaw centre is(215, 40, 35), handle points+y. The idle head's angled jaw rests on its support and the sleeve rests at height 10. Lot B shifts only the tool 20 mm toward -x, still on the same supports. Verify the tool stays within 1 mm/1 degree of its prepared pose for 5 seconds before enrollment.

Witness dial:50 mm diameter, 72 five-degree marks, pointer on cap. Model operational first grip 60 deg from reset (10 N/<1 mm criterion; geometric touch 50.4 deg); green begins 150 deg, nominal 4.5 Nm at 150 deg, red starts 153.44 deg. Model curve and 5.0 Nm rejection line are estimates. Physical dial marks come from the numeric three-specimen enrollment in README; 90 deg from first grip to 4.5 Nm, 0.3 Nm free drag and 50 N pull at 4.5 Nm were obtained by phone from a technician who works with this product (Nov 2026); the intermediate curve and damage onset remain estimates. Paint the cable's 4 mm green band centre 50 mm from its sheath end and 1 mm white pull witness 90 mm from that end; endpoint centre within +/-3 mm of the cap outer face. The model uses 36 mm reset cap-face-to-thread-end span from nominal H 27+GL 9; internal split remains an approximation.

One-time fixture materials are estimated under $10. Specimens are commercial stock. Resetting enrolled parts is estimated at 2-3 minutes; there is no casting, drying or curing. fixture.svg is the dimension drawing; it is not to scale. The hole, sleeve channel and tool proof loads must be checked against actual parts before physical trials.
