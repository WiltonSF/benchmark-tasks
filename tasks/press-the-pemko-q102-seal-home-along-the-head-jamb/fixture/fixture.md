# Frame-parts assembly fixture, version 1 (uncalibrated)

All dimensions mm. On the station bench build a reusable plywood assembly deck,
950 along y, 160 along x, 18 thick, centered at (280,0). Two timber battens
160  x 40  x 40 sit under it at y=+/-300. Deck underside z=40 and top z=58 above
the table. The full 812.8 x115.8875 jamb rests on the deck, x center=280.
No fixture touches, aligns or inserts the loose seal.

Two BESSEY CM30 clamps secure the back edge at y=+/-330. Maker opening 76.2,
throat 44.45; see linked manufacturer brochure in README. Columns x=371;
spindle/pad x=327. Lower jaw z=34, upper frame z=118 above table; the bottom
jaw reaches under the deck in the 40 mm gap. The top pad rests on the stop at z=89.75 above the table, not inside the lower
stop. Deck-plus-stop stack 49.75 mm
fits the opening. Envelope dimensions, not manufacturer CAD. No clamp passes
through the station bench. Fix the deck/battens once as a screw-built assembly.

Slot face x=265. Center is 25.4 above deck, width 3.175, depth 9.525. Board
thickness 19.05 and stop height 12.7 are estimates to enroll against purchased
parts. Drawing jamb.svg labels the local end section relative to deck top.

Prepare a commercial strip once to 812.8 +/-1. Mark 34 equal spans on the outer seal cover and number
every fifth. Start with right end 20 mm overhang, fin tip 6 mm short of slot.
The smooth block is 40 x 30 x 20, at (175,-190) or(175,-230) on the station table.
Commercial reuse requires visual inspection and force enrollment in README.
No per-trial printing, casting or curing is proposed. Reset time is unmeasured.
