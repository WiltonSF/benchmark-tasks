# Independent inspection protocol, version1

No physical inter-rater agreement has been measured. A real trial needs an overhead before/after macrophoto with a 1mm rule in the coupon plane, fixed oblique lighting from each of two opposite directions, and60fps strike video. Confirm a blank uncracked50x50x3mm annealed coupon and record its position against the vise before starting. The robot has180seconds.

Prepare a reusable reference from each actual08091 die: take a single full impression on a separate sample coupon with the same lighting, trace its complete groove centre-line, and retain the image and traced line. This one-time reference is not a per-trial casting or stencil. The simulator's approximate Gothic lines are not claimed to be the manufacturer's exact face mask. Physical calibration and two independent scorers on trial photographs remain required qualification work.

Rectify the scored photo from the four coupon corners; ruler determines mm/pixel (at least20pixels/mm). Register each digit's reference by translation and rotation only, never by scale or deformation, minimizing centre-line distance within0.15mm. Count a reference length as covered only where a visible continuous groove lies within0.15mm. At least90% total reference length and no uncovered run longer than0.5mm are needed for each intended digit. Extra overlap does not erase a missing groove. Two lighting views resolve shadow ambiguity; if they disagree, retake the photograph before grading. An additional displaced groove at least2mm long and at least0.5mm from all intended reference lines is an extra impression and permanently0. Video also makes a second distinct blow on any character permanently0, even if its grooves overlap exactly. A tap that leaves no observable groove earns nothing.

Every digit must lie within4mm of its designated centre(-12,0),(0,0),(12,0)mm in the coupon frame, and within20degrees of upright, for any partial credit. Wrong character or slot, stock displacement>3mm or tilt>5degrees, fresh visible crack>=2mm, hammer touching the punch hand or bare specimen, a die footprint slipping>0.5mm during its loaded strike, any second blow leaving an impression, or any extra groove as above:0 permanently. A poorly formed but otherwise intended first strike earns no credit for that digit; it cannot be repaired by striking again. Count complete intended digits for0.25,0.50,0.75; their spatial order is123 regardless of which is struck first. For1.0 all3 also need center x pitch12+/-1mm, baseline range<=1mm, rotation<=10degrees and tools back within15mm of their declared body-origin positions. All tools must be supported and visibly motionless, hands>=50mm from them and the specimen for1second at every nonzero level. A ruler measures positions; the photograph measures pitch, baseline and angle; the video judges release, stillness and whole-trial failures.

The simulation uses depth>=50micrometres as its visible-groove proxy and the same90%/0.5mm test. This depth is not proposed as a simple-tool physical measurement. Its relationship to visibility must be checked on the real brass. The present overstrike energy proxy0.40J represents coupon damage conservatively; no quantitative fracture law is claimed. An actual fresh crack is read from the photo. This limitation must remain in the fidelity text and report.
