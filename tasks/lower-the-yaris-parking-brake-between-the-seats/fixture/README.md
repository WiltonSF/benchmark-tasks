# Stock-lever fixture and passive grip support

The full-size original Yaris grip/button/pull rod/nut remain unmodified. The robot receives passive steel under-grip shoes, with no extra actuator or force allowance. A swivelling retained-nut yoke drives a guarded exact-spring cartridge behind the bench. Its manufactured dimensions, adjustment limits, complete load path, priced stock and required one-time fit/proof/curve enrollment are specified; physical proof has not been performed.

After enrollment the assembled fixture resets in minutes without a new specimen, cure or fabrication.

The same two exact springs now have a front 8 mm compression stop and total 22.1 mm stop; load-envelope-reference.json is an analytic design reference, not a measured OEM curve. Complete force enrollment remains required.

[Analytic progressive load/travel reference](load-envelope.svg) shows the declared model envelope and spring travel; it is not a physical measurement.
