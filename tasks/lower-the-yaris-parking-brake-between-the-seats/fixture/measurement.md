# Proposed physical measurement procedure

This is a procedure for a future qualified fixture, not a measured trial. Native simulation uses240 Hz contact observations and a50 ms holding-moment window. The manual procedure below establishes the enrolled mechanism, visible work and outcome. It does not reconstruct those internal numerical guards from video.

1. Before enrollment, inspect the OEM pawl, push rod, return spring, bracket, keyed adapter, cable terminations and anchors. Proof and enroll the complete load path as specified in README.md and dimensions.json. Use the calibrated VEVOR OCS-L scale directly in the cable route:88.7/551.7/1014.7/1477.6 N at0/8/16/24 degrees,0 to+5%. The spring bank stays connected throughout the trial. Record the carriage position at each enrollment angle so loss of preload can also be seen in the trial video.
2. With the pawl unloaded, enroll button force versus marked0/5/8 mm travel. The existing5-6 N at5 mm requirement is a specimen acceptance requirement, not an OEM measurement. Account for the scale's50 g division; do not accept a lower bound below5 N. A purchased lever that fails enrollment is not a qualified specimen. Separately enroll the dry coating for112 N tangential traction under80 N total pinch. Record all readings and instrument error before the timed trial; this is one-time qualification, not per-trial fabrication.
3. Film the protractor, button travel marks, separate hands, fixed bracket and spring carriage continuously. Show a real lift off the tooth before button operation and controlled lowering with the other hand holding the lever. A support hand merely following a falling lever is not controlled lowering. Button-return settling to the next tooth is allowed. If a view hides a relevant contact, retain an additional view; a hidden event cannot be certified from the end photograph.
4. Read the supported final angle on the protractor. Use a30 mm gauge block at the lever/button to establish the view's scale, remove it before the trial, and check both complete grippers remain at least30 mm clear for at least1 second. Apply measurement uncertainty conservatively; a nominal30 mm reading is insufficient if its error could cross the threshold. Button and lever must be still and mechanically supported, rather than held by a hand. Assign the same angle tiers as task.json.

This protocol separates enrollment, observed controlled manipulation and the photo/video outcome. The inline scale does not measure signed hand moment or continuous button-contact force. Claiming numerical equivalence to every native force/history guard would require a synchronized, calibrated contact-wrench procedure with stated uncertainty and included cost; that instrumentation is not yet specified. No physical force-gate validation, fabrication, reset timing or hardware result is claimed. Native scores must not be relabelled as physical measurements.
