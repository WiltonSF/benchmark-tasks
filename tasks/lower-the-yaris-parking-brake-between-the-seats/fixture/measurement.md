# Release-load and grip qualification required

Use a 2012-2019 Toyota Yaris with the central hand lever, noting year and rear drum/disc brakes. Park on level ground, transmission in Park or gear, wheels chocked; a qualified operator keeps the vehicle independently secured. Hook a calibrated hanging force gauge at the grip middle, square to the lever. From fully down set exactly 3,4,5,6 clicks and normal parking setting. At each setting apply ordinary light button pressure and pull up slowly until the button goes in; record the peak reading and lever angle. Repeat three times. Record kg or N (kg readings times 9.80665). This is the desired release pull, distinct from the 200 N application inspection.

Record application pull at each click, button force/travel with the pawl unloaded, pivot-to-grip distance, grip dimensions, lever angles and bottom console clearance. Photograph scale attachment and note resolution/calibration. The on-car phone figures in parameters.md now supply the nominal load basis. Enroll the complete fixture curve against those targets; do not infer a release ratio from atypical adapted controls.

The passive shoes replace friction reliance. Native validation uses zero friction on both grip and holding-finger partners, with contact carried by the feet below the original grip. Follow shoes.md for the 150 N per-shoe design calculation and physical proof/inspection procedure. A native zero-friction lift is simulation evidence, separate from actual steel-sandwich fit and shoe proof. Do not call an untested hardware attachment physically qualified.

Video scoring uses synchronized angle scale, returned button, whole lever and both complete hand surfaces, with a 30 mm reference and timestamps. Check supported final angle for 1 s with both hands 30 mm clear of the lever and button. A fall of 8 degrees within 0.1 s gives zero credit. No continuous signed contact-moment or button-force measurement is needed for scoring. Enrollment load/traction measurements remain required for construct fidelity.

Real video uses the button out witness mark and visible hand contact throughout descent. The 1.1 mm/2 mm per second button checks,0.25 degree unheld descent and 5 ms gaps are simulation-only integrity checks, not claimed 30 fps measurements. Real-robot duty-cycle enrollment must retain stock 27/7 N m and 50 N caps, record motor temperature/current faults and reject a cycle that faults; the 27 N m peak is not a published nine-second thermal rating.
