# On-car release figures and model derivation

On-car technician figures (by phone, Nov 2026; source in the task README), a 2018 Toyota Yaris hatchback XP130: perpendicular grip release pull 54 N at four clicks / 210 mm radius; approximately proportional to click count; normal five clicks, about five degrees per click. Slack return 3.9 N. Button 7.4 N through 8 mm.

START_DEG=25; PITCH_DEG=5; RELEASE_PULL_N=67.5; LOAD_RISE_N_PER_DEG=2.7; GRIP_CENTRE_R=0.210. Reported lower torque is 0.210*max(3.9,2.7*angle_deg). The simulator uses the larger of that torque and the declared progressive-spring reference:54.48 N/mm per TOHATSU spring, estimated effective rod radius 35 mm, estimated total preload 1 mm, front compression stop 8 mm. At total spring compression x, rod force is 27240*(x+max(0, x-0.016)) N; the corresponding reference torque is 0.035*cos(theta)*force. These reference geometry/preload values are design estimates, not Yaris readings. Full curve enrollment must fit the reported lower bound and the declared simulator upper envelope; no inferred cam certificate is used. The maximum reference compression through 36.5 degrees is 21.819 mm, below the 22.0 mm operating limit and 22.1 mm hard stop. Values at other clicks follow the reported proportionality, assuming zero clicks at the down stop; they are not additional readings.

Estimated 0.80 kg lever gravity is additional conservative resistance. Button K=0; constant passive return 7.5962 N=7.4+0.020*9.81, damping 2 N s/m. All inward stroke resists at least 7.4 N even with gravity assistance; release requires 8 mm. This is a conservative envelope, not an unreported OEM spring curve. Full-stroke potential0.0607696 J before gravity.

The holding fingers and stock grip use matching zero-friction materials; passive shoes carry the load by bearing. Each shoe is modelled at 50 g including fasteners, with updated COM/inertia. Mass, access-envelope dimensions and lever profile remain estimates; actual shoe/jaw attachment compatibility still requires inspection/proof. The rejected 68 N application/release ratio and 50 N m application-force model are withdrawn. No scoring threshold has been relaxed to credit a failed demo.

The supplied embodiment sets 27/7 N m and 50 N finger caps. [DAMIAO maker specification](https://www.damiaokeji.com/index.php?c=show&id=100), opened Nov 24, lists 9 N m rated/27 N m peak for the DM4340; no nine-second peak-duration allowance is provided. Combined implicitPD-plus-gravity caps are enforced in motors/ and verified each native step.

The 0.210 m constant is the reported scale attachment radius used to convert measured external pull to resisting torque. The modeled grip span 170–272 mm (centre 221 mm) and its diameter/profile are independent geometric estimates; they are not a second measurement of the reported grip middle. The demonstration holds at 228 mm, within that stock-grip approximation. Original OEM grip/button poses must be registered during physical enrollment; no exact hardware replay result is claimed.
