# Passive bearing shoes on the stock nylon fingers

The maker identifies Linear 4310 tips as nylon plastic with rubber gripping surfaces. No nylon grade or allowable is inferred. Two passive steel L shoes support the unmodified OEM grip by bearing. No actuator or effort allowance is added. Matching zero/max friction on both holding fingers and the grip removes traction dependence in simulation. Native results are modeled-geometry evidence; physical assembly proof remains required.

## Strength screen and enrollment

The unchanged proof envelope is 150 N radial load plus 5 N along the lever axis, at either distal toe corner for 60 s. Keep 150 N and 3.60 N m as minimum references when comparing native demand, requiring at least 1.5 times the final finger resultant and attachment moment. Require the same margin against 5 N axial demand. This qualifies hardware enrollment; it is not a force-scoring gate.

The 24 mm A36 foot cantilever has 61.37 MPa reference vertical bending at 150 N; the corner/torsion screen is below 90 MPa versus 250 MPa nominal A36 yield. The 6.8 mm bolt group and 3 mm minimum effective bearing-reaction arm bound axial screw demand below 1.27 kN. A335 N loose transverse estimate is superseded by the exact vector bound below 268 N; use 300 N for bush design. The uniformly round 4.39 mm minimum bush,3.02 mm maximum bore and 6.27 mm maximum length give combined axial/cantilever/shear von Mises demand below 457 MPa against the required 600 MPa received yield. No reduced throat or uncertain notch factor is needed. Web socket bearing is below 137 MPa; 3 mm full A36 thread shear below 121 MPa using the conservative area 0.5*pi*2.24*3 mm², versus 144 MPa nominal shear yield (the simpler major-diameter area gives 90 MPa).

Require no more than 0.02 mm relative lateral steel-stack displacement during the complete proof, measured with a 0.001 mm resolving workshop indicator. Select fits jointly; taking every clearance at its maximum is not guaranteed to pass. Conditional on this measured bound, a conservative7.5 mm screw span with both end rotations restrained gives 478 MPa bending (3*E*d*delta/L², E200 GPa, root 2.24 mm). With the axial bound and 75 N direct shear, combined screw demand is below 801 MPa versus class 12.9 proof 970 MPa. The backing plate's paired bending/socket screen is below 255 MPa versus the required 600 MPa received 1144 yield. These are conditional design screens, not performed physical tests.

Use bearing blue to require at least 60 mm² effective hard-nylon support on each side of the bolt line, centroids at least 3 mm from it, and no unsupported backing bridge over 6.8 mm. Maximum design bearing demand is 21.2 MPa; no nylon allowable is invented. Proof each complete prepared original finger, plates, bushes and shoe in a rigid shop jig using the normal original finger mounting fasteners and mating face. Do not hang the proof from the extended live arm. Apply the radial and axial load cases at both distal ends, then proof the pair on the original grip in an independently supported lever jig at the actual radius. Reject any crack, permanent set, loose screw, rocking, nylon indentation, socket movement, excess displacement or lost flush seating. Recheck gripper travel and witnesses. No physical proof is claimed.
